The Garden
Table of Tansen

It started with one good plate of sekuwa and a garden worth lingering in. Today Royal Inn is the town's favourite gathering place—a lantern-lit terrace strung with prayer flags, charcoal smoke on the air, and the easy kind of hospitality that turns an hour into a whole evening.
